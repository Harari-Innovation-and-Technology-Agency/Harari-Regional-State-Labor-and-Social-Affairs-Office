About Us

Learn about our vision, mission, and commitment to the community

Our Vision

To be an organization that provides first-class services in the Harare region, where workers' rights are protected, appropriate working conditions are provided, and social benefits are promoted, and to encourage the workforce and community to work together to achieve overall development.

Our Mission

Ensuring equal opportunities and full participation of persons with disabilities, providing support and care for the elderly and promoting their participation, and providing various supports to citizens living in poverty, vulnerability, and exclusion due to various economic and social problems.

Our Core Values

  • Loyalty and trustworthiness
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Submission to the rule of law
  • Efficiency, in the work you do
  • Serving the people and citizens without discrimination
  • All work is noble, and serving is an honor.

Our Responsibilities

The Harari Regional Labour and Social Affairs Office has its responsibilities as a basis for the adjustment of workers' rights, welfare and wages issues in the region and to provide social development services on a more affordable basis. These responsibilities include the welfare of employees, making demands created in the workplace, managing social welfare services, as well as implementing plans and operations that allow community groups in various sectors in the region to use (disabled people, elders, children, women and youth).
